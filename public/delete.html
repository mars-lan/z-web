Delete your data

How you remove your 18 Browser data depends on whether you signed in.

If you never signed in

Your data stays on your device. Uninstall 18 Browser to remove it.

If you signed in

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. That permanently deletes your account and the data synced to our servers.

Uninstalling the app afterward clears anything left on the device.
